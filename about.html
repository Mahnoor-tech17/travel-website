We plan trips people actually remember

Wayfarer started in 2019 as a two-person side project: a shared spreadsheet of the best beaches, temples, and cities we'd visited ourselves. Today it's grown into a small team of former guides and travel writers who still hand-check every recommendation before it goes live.

We don't sell tours or take commissions from hotels. Our only job is to point you toward places worth the trip — and tell you honestly when somewhere isn't.
